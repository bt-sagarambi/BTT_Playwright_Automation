Overview
The Revenue Over Time Widget shows you the real time revenue, page views, orders and session
activity for any and all traffic segments visiting your site. Hover your mouse over the graph and you'll
get a tool-tip reading of the numbers at this time.

Find Widget
Once you enter the Add a Widget menu, you will see a variety of filter options on top. One way to find
the Revenue Over Time Widget is to use the filter options listed below.
