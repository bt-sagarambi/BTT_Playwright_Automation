Widget Overview
The DEO Anomalies and Site Opportunity Widget is viewable on the DEO homepage.
This widget is made up of machine learning that is constantly monitoring your site for issues, known
as our anomaly detection.
When it detects an issue, it will automatically move that issue to the top of the widget and prioritize
it by Severity (Critical, Significant, Normal, or Minor) and create a card in the issue tracker.
If you want further information on the issue, you can click on the down carrot, and an expanded view
will show what the issue description was.

If you click Actions -> View Details, it will link you to the location in the portal with data for the given
issue. For example, if a slowdown was detected for a particular domain, this would take you to the
Domain Details page. 
If you perceive the issue as not important or perhaps it's already fixed, you can archive or dismiss
the issue within the Actions menu. 
 
 
Organizing DEO Page
There are only two stationary widgets on the DEO page: Blue Triangle Scores and the DEO Anomalies
and Site Opportunities widgets. You can move any other widget by dragging them around to
customize your view. You can also hide any other widget by clicking the X button in the top right.
